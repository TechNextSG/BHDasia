About the Training

ICF-accredited TRE™ Module 1, in Lisboa

This ICF-accredited TRE™ Module 1 belongs to the only TRE™ training in the world that carries ICF CCEUs: 21 CCEUs (16 Resource Development + 5 Core Competencies). TRE™ was developed by Dr. David Berceli and is designed to engage the body's own neurogenic tremoring reflex, which many people find helps them let go of held stress, build resilience and regulate more deeply.

Module 1 qualifies you to use TRE™ in your own personal practice, and counts as Module 1 of the Global TRE™ Provider Certification. Add Modules 2 and 3 and you become a Certified Global TRE™ Provider, accredited to offer TRE™ to individuals and groups.

About the 21 ICF CCEUs: the ICF-accredited Module 1 includes an additional 4 hours, “Self-Care & Presence for Coaches” (Day 3, 30 May, 14:00–18:00), which is mandatory to attend if you want to get the 21 CCEs. Not interested in the ICF CCEs? Attend 28 May (all day), 29 May (all day) and 30 May (morning).

A small group, limited to 25 participants, led by a Global TRE™ Certifying Trainer with an assisting provider, in English.

Who it is for

Who this Module 1 is for

  • Coaches (in training, or at ACC, PCC or MCC level) looking for an ICF-accredited somatic tool
  • Therapists, psychologists and healthcare professionals growing a trauma-informed practice
  • Yoga teachers, bodyworkers and somatic practitioners who want to add neuroscience-informed tools
  • Anyone experiencing chronic stress or burnout and ready for a body-led approach
  • Anyone considering the full Global TRE™ Provider Certification in Lisboa (Modules 1, 2 & 3)
