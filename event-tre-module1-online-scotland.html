About TRE™

ICF-accredited TRE™ Module 1, online

TRE™ (Tension & Trauma Releasing Exercises) was developed by Dr. David Berceli. It activates the body's natural neurogenic tremoring mechanism, a reflex that releases stored stress and trauma, builds resilience and promotes deep regulation.

Module 1 qualifies you to use TRE™ for your personal practice. With Modules 2 and 3 you become a Certified Global TRE™ Provider. This is the only TRE™ training anywhere in the world that carries ICF CCEUs: 21 CCEUs (16 Resource Development + 5 Core Competencies), issued on attendance and completion of the full 2.5 days.

A small group, limited to 15 participants, led by a Global TRE™ Certifying Trainer with an assisting provider, in English.

Who it is for

Who this Module 1 is for

  • Coaches (in training, or at ACC, PCC or MCC level) looking for an ICF-accredited somatic tool
  • Therapists, psychologists and healthcare professionals expanding a trauma-informed practice
  • Yoga and Pilates teachers, bodyworkers and somatic practitioners
  • Anyone with chronic stress or burnout who is ready for a body-led approach
  • Anyone considering the full Global TRE™ Provider Certification
