About the Certification

Become a Certified Global TRE™ Provider in Lisboa

The complete Global TRE™ Provider Certification in Lisboa, Portugal: three in-person modules from May to October 2027, each with online TRE™ personal sessions or supervisions, and 21 ICF CCEs (16 Resource Development + 5 Core Competencies) for Module 1.

TRE™ was developed by Dr. David Berceli and is designed to engage the body's own neurogenic tremoring reflex, which many people find helps them let go of held stress, build resilience and regulate more deeply. Module 1 qualifies you to use TRE™ in your own personal practice; with Modules 2 and 3 you become a Certified Global TRE™ Provider, accredited to offer TRE™ to individuals and groups.

A small group, limited to 25 participants, led by a Global TRE™ Certifying Trainer with an assisting provider, in English.

Who it is for

Who this Certification is for

  • Coaches (in training, or at ACC, PCC or MCC level) looking for an ICF-accredited somatic tool
  • Therapists, psychologists or healthcare professionals expanding a trauma-informed practice
  • Yoga teachers, Pilates teachers, bodyworkers or somatic practitioners
  • Anyone experiencing chronic stress or burnout and ready for a body-led approach
What is Included

Your Certification Package

  • Full certification: Modules 1, 2 and 3, with 4 online personal sessions (Module 1) and 4 online individual supervisions each for Modules 2 and 3
  • 21 ICF CCEs (16 Resource Development + 5 Core Competencies, ICF-accredited) for Module 1
  • 8.5 training days, including the Day 3 afternoon workshop for coaches in Module 1
  • Global recognition: the Global TRE™ Certification Program is recognised worldwide
  • Small group, limited to 25 participants, led by a Global TRE™ Certifying Trainer with an assisting provider, in English
