About TRE™

Become a Certified TRE™ Provider

TRE™ is a body-led series of simple, powerful exercises to release stress, tension and trauma and build resilience. It safely activates the body's natural reflex of shaking or vibrating, which helps calm the nervous system and release muscular tension — allowing the body to naturally return to a balanced state.

The Global TRE™ Provider Certification program allows certificate holders to offer TRE™ to the general public in both individual and group settings. The 2027 cohort is co-taught by Isabelle Claus Teixeira and Simba Stenqvist.

Only one cohort per year in Singapore — dates for 2027 are confirmed below.

Who it is for

For New Students

The Growth Package is for new students who want the full Global TRE™ Provider Certification — Modules 1–3, all online personal sessions and supervisions, ICF credits and three self-paced bonus programmes.

What is Included

Everything in the Growth Package

  • Modules 1–3 in Singapore (27–28 Feb, 3–4 Jul, 30–31 Oct 2027)
  • All online personal sessions and supervisions
  • ICF CCEs: 21 for Module 1 with the Growth Package (CCEs pending confirmation for Module 2), released on full attendance of the relevant module
  • Three self-paced bonus programmes (value S$1,290): Grounding, Verbal Interventions, Internal Alchemy

Bonus programmes

Three self-paced online programmes, together valued at S$1,290, come with this package. Grounding unlocks once Instalment 2 is received (due by 3 June 2027). Verbal Interventions and Internal Alchemy unlock once Instalment 3 is received (due by 30 September 2027). If you pay the full package price at registration, all three unlock after Module 2 (3–4 July 2027).

Payment Terms

How Payment Works

  • Payment is by credit card only, via Stripe (Odoo checkout). Card charges are added on top of every instalment.
  • The full package price is owed once the first instalment is paid.
  • Prefer to pay in full? You can pay the full package price at registration instead of three instalments. Card charges still apply.
  • Instalment 1 is due on registration — by 31 Jan 2027 at the early-bird price, by 25 Feb 2027 at the regular price.
  • Instalment 2 is issued after Module 1 and due by 3 June 2027. Instalment 3 is issued after Module 2 and due by 30 September 2027.
  • Extra online personal sessions or supervisions (any package): S$190 per 60-minute online session.
  • ICF credits are included in the Growth Package.
  • All ticket sales are final — tickets are non-refundable and non-cancellable.
